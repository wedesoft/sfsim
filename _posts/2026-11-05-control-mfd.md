I have released version 0.37-1 of [sfsim][1].

![flight control display](/sfsim/pics/controls.jpg)

This release adds a new flight control display showing the positions of the ailerons, elevator, and rudder.
It lets you see at a glance how your control inputs move the surfaces responsible for roll, pitch, and yaw.
